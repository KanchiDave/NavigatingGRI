Materiality matrix

GRI 3 ranks topics by the significance of the organization's impacts on the economy, environment and people (severity and likelihood). Use stakeholder concern to test that ranking. It should not replace it. Shaded cells score 7 or more combined.

These are example scores for a generic mid-size organization. Replace them with scores from your own materiality workshop.

The GRI 2 general disclosures don't depend on materiality, so start them in phase 2. Only the topic disclosures wait until the material topics are agreed.
